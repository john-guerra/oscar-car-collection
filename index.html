About This Collection

This collection represents a 30-year journey through the golden age of automobiles, meticulously assembled piece by piece.

With a focus on models representing vehicles up to 1960, the collection showcases the craftsmanship of renowned manufacturers like Matchbox, Lansdowne, and Gowland & Gowland.

Each model is carefully selected for its historical significance, quality of craftsmanship, and the story it tells about automotive history. From elegant touring cars to legendary racing machines, every piece in this collection has been chosen with passion and dedication.

The collection continues to grow, with new discoveries being added regularly. Welcome to explore and share in the appreciation of these miniature masterpieces.
